# Data_mining_project
Analysis of product returns from a shop with a view to improving the company's strategic decisions, using in-depth exploratory analyses and the application of our logistic regression model.

Application of the model:

--> Model chosen: logistic regression was decisive
due to its simplicity, efficiency and ease of interpretation

--> Anticipates returns: identifies customers
likely to return products, based on their history and
frequent reasons (such as ‘unmet expectations’
or ‘regret’)

--> Manages at-risk customers: enables the offering of
personalised solutions and prevents logistical issues

--> Optimises the catalogue: detects products with a
high risk of return in order to adjust marketing, stock and communication strategies
